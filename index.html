How I Work

MY
DESIGN
process.

01
DISCOVER

Deep-dive into user needs through interviews, surveys, and competitor analysis. I don't start designing until I fully understand the problem and the people it affects.

02
DEFINE

Synthesising research into clear problem statements, personas, and user journeys. Strategy meets empathy — turning raw insights into design direction.

03
DESIGN

From low-fidelity wireframes to pixel-perfect high-fidelity screens. Iteration is constant — nothing is precious, everything serves the user.

04
DELIVER

Interactive prototypes, developer handoff, and design system documentation. I stay involved through implementation to ensure the vision lands exactly as designed.
